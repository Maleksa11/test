Privacy and storage

Your watch history and interests are stored in this browser only and are not encrypted by the app. Search terms and requested video IDs are sent to external video search instances. There is no account system or shared server-side history in this version. Anyone using the same browser profile can see that profile's local history.

Install as an app

To open it like an app: on iPhone, tap Share in Safari, then “Add to Home Screen”. On Android, tap ⋮ then “Add to Home screen”.
